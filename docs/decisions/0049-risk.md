# 0049. Risk: a register people keep, acceptance that ends, and a history nobody edits

- **Decision:** Proposed
- **Implementation:** In progress
- **Where:** `risk` keeps the risk register, vendor reviews, links to controls, findings and incidents in other products, a heat map, the controls of ISO 27001 and SOC 2 that treat each risk, and a history of every change (`ghcr.io/openasy/risk`).
- **Priority:** High
- **Area:** Architecture

## Context

A risk register is where a team writes down what could go wrong, how bad it would be and what it is doing about it, and where an auditor looks for proof that somebody decided. Most registers are spreadsheets. They work until two people edit them, until nobody remembers why a risk was accepted, or until the acceptance from two years ago is still quietly in force.

The other products find things: vulnerabilities, failed controls, incidents. Some of them will not be fixed soon, and that is a decision too, which belongs in the register rather than in a finding that stays open forever.

## Decision

- **A risk is scored by likelihood times impact**, each 1 to 5, in bands: low under 5, medium from 5, high from 10, critical from 17. A treatment (mitigate, transfer, accept, avoid) can give a residual score, where the risk is after it.
- **Accepting a risk is a decision somebody answers for.** It needs a reason, an approver and an end at most 366 days away, and only admins make it. When it ends the risk is review-due until someone accepts it again or changes it. No acceptance lasts forever.
- **Every risk and vendor has a review interval** (90 and 365 days by default). Overdue reviews show on the register and in `/metrics`.
- **History is written by the product and kept by the database.** Every change is recorded with who made it and what changed, and database triggers refuse to change or delete a history row. It goes only with its risk or vendor; to keep the record, a risk is closed rather than deleted.
- **Other products open risks** with an API token, setting an external source and reference so that one problem is one risk however often it is reported (ADR 0023). Links to controls, findings and incidents are references only; nothing is fetched from the other product.
- **Vendors** are kept with the data they hold, their criticality, the state of their questionnaire, a rating, the risks they bring and links to their documents. Documents are links; nothing is stored or fetched.

## Consequences

- The register is a record, so nothing is deleted on a schedule.
- The bands are fixed. Teams with their own matrix wait for configurable bands, and the API returns likelihood and impact so they can compute their own.
- The controls of ISO 27001:2022 Annex A and the SOC 2 Trust Services Criteria are built in, by number and a short name; the standards' text is not copied. A risk links to the controls that treat it, each change is in its history, and a control that treats no risk shows as such. Evidence for a control, and frameworks beyond these two, wait for a compliance product.
